About Mini Games Hub

Mini Games Hub is a collection of fun, simple browser games made with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

You can play the games directly in your browser without installing anything.

Your scores are saved in your browser, so you can try to beat your personal best!

📖

How to Play

Quick instructions for every game.

🐍

Snake

Use the arrow keys or WASD to move. Eat the food and avoid the walls and your own snake.

❌⭕

Tic-Tac-Toe

Take turns placing X and O. The first player to get three in a row wins.

✊

Rock Paper Scissors

Choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, and Paper beats Rock.

🧠

Memory Game

Flip two cards at a time. Find all eight matching pairs using as few moves as possible.
